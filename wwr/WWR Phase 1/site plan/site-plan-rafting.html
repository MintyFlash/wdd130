Overview

Purpose

This is the site for our company, Dry Oar, where people can go to see the rafting trips we have to offer. We want this website because we want more people to know about our company so they can see what we're all about. If they are interested they can book white water rafting trips with us too, and maybe even share the site with others. If the site works for what we need it to do, we can become the top rafting company in Idaho, when people search for rafting trips in Idaho, the first thing they will see is our website, and that will be the one they click on without hesitation because it's at the top of the results. This could also turn out to be a huge financial benefit for us, because if more people know about our company, we can increase our profits. There will be huge social benefits for us too, we'll be largely respected and talked about across Idaho because of how people have experienced our services

Audience

We are obviously targeting Adventure seekers, nature lovers, and families; The main people we know will get on the site will be adults, like parents, individuals, or event planners We need to keep these people in mind and make this site simple for them it needs to be straight to the point offering out of the gates the things they need like info on all the different rivers, different experiences and what they have to offer, available dates, the pricing and of course the full direct booking process right there to access. This will keep them on the site, because the content is what they really need, and is offered to the user instantly. Speaking of which, a need that is often unmet is info available to read on the fly, instead the user needs to dig through a lot of subpages to find what they actually want, so we need a way for info to be available either the moment they get on the site, or that you have to go through as few subpages possible They will be accessing our site through either a computer or phone, you never know what they will use, so we need to account for both, the site needs to display well on both types of devices so development must focus on optimizing for displaying on both device types
